# RoTrade privacy policy

RoTrade runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and the developer never receives your data.

## What RoTrade reads
- **Pages on roblox.com** you visit (trade, catalog, profile and inventory pages), to show values and tools on them.
- **Roblox APIs** (trades, inventory, catalog, resale data, thumbnails, users), using your existing Roblox login in the browser, to show your trades, inventory and market data.
- **Rolimons public APIs** for item values and player info.

## What RoTrade stores
Settings, your trade history, wishlist, value snapshots and your trade ad are saved in Chrome's extension storage on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by RoTrade.

## What RoTrade sends, and only if you turn it on
- **Rolimons trade ads:** when you enable auto-posting, your ad (item IDs and request tags) and Roblox user ID are sent to Rolimons, using your own Rolimons login.
- **Discord webhook:** when you add a webhook URL, the notifications you pick (trade details, pictures of trades, value changes, ad results) are sent to that Discord webhook. The URL stays on your computer.

RoTrade does not sell, share or use your data for advertising, and does not collect analytics.
